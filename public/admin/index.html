How this editor works Login with GitHub to add rides and notes

Use this page on https://thenewsoulsearchers.de/admin/ (not www). First click the gold Login with GitHub in this bar. Then use the gold Save in the You are here bar (and again at the top of the bottom-right stack). That writes to GitHub. Delete this sits beside Save in that same bar. The top Publish menu stays open so you can pick Publish now if you want. The editor is the form only (no preview sitting on top of the fields). After save, open the ride or note page itself and hard-refresh (Cmd+Shift+R). GitHub already has the text and photos. The public Worker can sit on an old build, so an empty page after Publish is the site being behind, not a lost save.

Photos: JPEG from the Mac or phone. Click Upload, pick the file (yes, from your computer — that is how it lands on the site), then the gold save button. Same on rides and ride notes. First photo sits at the top. Extra photos sit in the text. The picture button puts a photo where you are typing. If the editor thumb looks broken, wait a second — we load it from GitHub. iPhone HEIC is a broken thumb here. Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible, or export a JPEG from Photos.

Shots: one photo and a line. They show on Now, newest first.

Mail and imprint: use the gold button at the bottom-right. A form opens above it on the right. Type your email, street, and city, then Save mail and imprint. Then open Contact and Imprint and hard-refresh.

Instagram: open Site, then Instagram. Put the username, then paste the latest photo’s Share link.

Where you are: the big gold bar under the brand says THIS WEEK, STORES AROUND THE WORLD, RIDES, and so on. Sidebar names also turn red. Click the section you want so you never get lost.

Rides: ＋ Ride asks for a name, then opens the file. Write the story, then scroll past Draft — there is UPLOAD PHOTOS right underneath. Cover first, then more shots in the story. Click a ride so it turns green, then − Ride.

Ride notes: open Ride notes, then a note. Same idea — after Draft comes UPLOAD PHOTOS. Click Upload, pick a JPEG from your Mac, then gold Save this note. Not HEIC. Big phone photos are fine; GitHub shrinks them after save. If Upload ever bounces you out of the note, hard-refresh and try again.

Stores & This week: open the shop or note, then Add photos in the You are here bar. Shift-click every JPEG in the Mac window. Each one gets a light colour and crispness pass, then they land together. Then gold Save. A store holds 24, a week note 40. Not HEIC. To take a post off the site, open it and tap Delete this beside Save.

Friends: open Friends, then New Friend. Name, optional website, a short thank-you line, optional logo. Gold Save this friend. They show on Friends. Turn off Draft when you want them public.

Shop: open Shop, then New Product. Name, short text, optional photo, optional limit line (like 50 worldwide). Gold Save this product. People email henrik@thenewsoulsearchers.de from Shop. No cart. Old /patches link goes to Shop.

Looks: after login, the gold Looks pill in the bottom-right stack (with Mail and Save). Private. Not linked on the public site.
